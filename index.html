What Are Berry Services?

Explore Berry Clinic’s range of advanced aesthetic treatments, tailored to your individual needs to enhance your natural beauty and achieve harmonious, natural-looking results.

Facial Plastic Surgery

Rhinoplasty, Deep Plane Facelift, Blepharoplasty, Otoplasty, Brow Lift, Chin Augmentation

Our consultant-led facial plastic surgery in London is designed around restraint, precision and natural outcomes.
Never a template. Surgery takes place in fully accredited Harley Street facilities with consultant anaesthetists, and each journey is supported from first conversation through to final review.
If you are considering facial surgery and want results that look like you, only refreshed, our team will guide you through suitability, recovery and realistic expectations at every stage.

Body Contouring

Liposuction, Abdominoplasty (Tummy Tuck), Brazilian Butt Lift, Brachioplasty (Arm Lift), Thigh Lift

Body contouring is built for patients who have reached a stable weight but want to refine shape, remove stubborn fat or address loose skin after pregnancy or significant weight loss.
Our body contouring procedures are performed by consultant surgeons using techniques chosen to suit your anatomy and goals. Every plan is personalised, with clear guidance on scarring, downtime and long-term results.

Non-Surgical Aesthetics

Anti-Wrinkle Injections, Dermal Fillers, Laser Skin Resurfacing, Microneedling, Medical Chemical Peels

Our non-surgical aesthetics treatments offer subtle, doctor-led refinement without surgery or significant downtime. At Beery, every treatment is delivered by qualified experienced specialists using premium, evidence-based products and technology.
We focus on natural-looking results that preserve expression and skin health, with thorough consultations to confirm suitability and realistic expectations. Whether you are new to aesthetics or looking to refine an existing routine, Berry clinic provides a calm, considered approach to non-surgical rejuvenation.

Hair Restoration

FUE Hair Transplant, FUT Hair Transplant, PRP Hair Therapy, Eyebrow Transplant, Beard Transplant

Berry Clinic combines surgical precision with a genuinely natural approach to hairline design.Each hair treatment is tailored to your pattern of hair loss, donor availability and long-term goals.
Every procedure is performed by experienced surgeons with an emphasis on dense, undetectable hairlines and safe, comfortable recovery. Whether you are addressing early thinning or rebuilding a fuller hairline, our team will help you understand the options, timelines and results with complete transparency.
